The border beam in sharedUI/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/letta/mobile/ui/components/beam/
(Modifier.lettaBorderBeam, its shader source, Canvas fallback and palette maths, with the
platform shaders in androidMain and skikoMain) is ported to Kotlin Multiplatform from
border-beam-android, https://github.com/ismnoiet/border-beam-android
(commit 0855cf96819203fa803c4f3b5be1657e8b4620d7, 2026-11-12).

Changes: the Android-only parts (android.provider.Settings, LocalContext, Build checks inside
the modifier, a composed modifier) are replaced by a Modifier.Node that reads the shared
reduced-motion preference and pauses off screen; the AGSL source is shared with Skia SkSL for
desktop and web; the Canvas fallback caches its outline; colours and sizes come from design tokens.
