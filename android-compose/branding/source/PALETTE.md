# Baken Final Brand Kit (`baken.ca`)

- **Mark:** **03 Basalt Planes** — two river-worn leaning basalt stones, subtle `b` silhouette, verified flush ground baseline (`y=168`), `0` overlap, `5px` min / `10px` median seam at 200×200.
- **Micro optical cut (16–24 px):** dedicated bolder, wider-seam cut (`svg/baken-mark-micro-*.svg`, `android/ic_notification.xml`, `web/favicon-16.png`) verified `>= 1 px` separation at 16×16 px and 24×24 dp.
- **Wordmark:** **Plus Jakarta Sans Bold (700)** (SIL OFL, bundled in `fonts/`), tight `-3.5` tracking, lowercase `baken` / `baken.ca`.
- **Launcher Icon Policy:**
  - **Android Adaptive Icon:** **Flat two-path vector** (`android/ic_launcher_*.xml`) — preserves Material You monochrome themed icons cleanly.
  - **Desktop / Web / Play Store hero:** **Lit Basalt** (`svg/baken-app-icon-lit.svg`).
- **Palette:** **01 North Sea Buoy** (non-cream, WCAG verified)
